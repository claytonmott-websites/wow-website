The destination

A world-class IPTMS, owned by the people who operate it.

Taxis, buses, e-hailing, scholar transport and township delivery on one Integrated Public Transport Management System, unlocking prosperity through digitalisation.

  • Every mode, one system

    Shared rails, live fleet visibility, cashless fares on any phone and one wallet across the whole journey.

  • Ownership as infrastructure

    The platform, the wallet, the data and the revenue belong to the co-operative the industry built.

  • The national template

    Organised industry first, technology second, prosperity third. A blueprint for province after province.
