Who it’s for

Built for everyone who gets in.

Owners, drivers, commuters, co-ops, brands and government each get something different from WOW. Find your part.

Taxi owners

Earn more from the fleet you already run.

WOW turns the fuel you buy, the trips you run and the records you keep into money and leverage.

Get involved
  • Fuel rebates

    Collective buying power earns rebates at participating sites, paid back through your co-op. WOW Fuel

  • Your fleet, on screen

    Live GPS, onboard cameras and fuel tracking, so theft has nowhere to hide. Cashless + Track

  • Records the bank will accept

    Every recorded trip is proof of income and a path to vehicle finance. Cashless + Track

  • Instant, free payouts

    Your share lands in your WOW wallet in seconds, split exactly as the co-op agreed. Wallet & Payments

Drivers

Less cash. More control. Your own platform.

Carry less cash, get paid faster and earn in the quiet hours between peaks.

Get involved
  • Safer cashless fares

    Card, app, QR or USSD. Less cash in the vehicle means less risk on the road. Cashless + Track

  • A wallet that pays instantly

    Wallet-to-wallet transfers settle in seconds and cost nothing. Wallet & Payments

  • Earn between peaks

    E-hailing and township deliveries on the industry’s own platform. WOW-Go

  • Data that doesn’t expire

    Affordable SIMs sold at the rank, with data that stays yours until you use it. WOW Mobile

Commuters

Pay, connect and win on the ride you take.

WOW makes every trip simpler and more rewarding, on the phone you already own.

Get involved
  • Pay on any phone

    No cash needed. Pay by card, app, QR or USSD, even on the simplest handset. Cashless + Track

  • Free WiFi on board

    Ride connected on taxis and at ranks with WOW-Fi. WOW-Fi

  • Win real prizes

    Riding, topping up and spending earn LuckyX entries. Never bought, never gambling. LuckyX

  • Scholars get home safely

    Parents get an alert when their child boards and arrives. Scholar Transport

Associations & co-ops

Your co-op. Your rules. Enforced.

WOW records the structure your members built and runs it automatically, on every transaction.

Get involved
  • Membership on the record

    Every operator, vehicle and driver registered under your co-operative. Connect + Agent

  • Splits enforced in code

    Operator, association and co-op shares run exactly as you set them. Wallet & Payments

  • Help at your rank

    WOW Agents register members, top up wallets and train people where they work. Connect + Agent

  • Rebates flow back to you

    Fuel savings return through the co-operative structure you own. WOW Fuel

Brands & sponsors

Reach 10,7 million riders a day.

Minibus taxis carry more South Africans than any other channel. WOW lets brands reach them in ways commuters welcome.

Get involved
  • Advertise on WOW-Fi

    Reach commuters on the free WiFi they choose, on taxis and at ranks. WOW-Fi

  • Sponsor LuckyX prizes

    Fund rewards that commuters earn by riding, topping up and spending. LuckyX

  • Connect to rank retail

    Drive commuter wallets to merchants in the taxi economy. Cashless Merchants

Government

Accountable public transport, from the rank up.

WOW gives departments and municipalities verified data and a formal partner that represents the industry.

Get involved
  • Verified scholar transport

    Biometric proof of every trip, with ghost-learner claims designed out. Scholar Transport

  • An integrated transport system

    Taxis, buses and e-hailing on one platform, with live fleet visibility. Cashless + Track

  • One organised counterpart

    A provincial co-operative with mandate, legitimacy and reach.

  • A national template

    A model built in the Eastern Cape, ready to replicate province by province.
