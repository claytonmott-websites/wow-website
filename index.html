Why WOW

Organised from within. Impossible to copy.

Technology alone never worked in this industry. WOW starts with the thing no fintech, bank or corporate could buy or build: the industry itself, formally organised.

  • Adoption solved before deployment

    Collective ownership and stakeholder endorsement across the whole market, before a single device is installed.

  • Distribution, legitimacy, mandate

    Every region, association and operator in the province is connected to the co-operative that owns WOW.

  • Its own financial rails

    A regulated digital wallet and payment rails, owned rather than rented.
